## Technical qualifications clarified

- A diode's forward voltage depends on current and temperature; 0.7 V is an approximation, not an exact turn-on threshold.
- Schottky diodes avoid minority-carrier recovery, but still have capacitive switching current.
- Inductor saturation reduces inductance; its behavior depends on the core.
- The MOSFET Miller plateau explanation focuses on gate-drain charge and switching loss.
- Phase lead from an op-amp zero is qualified as a left-half-plane zero.
- A sine input producing the same-frequency sine output is described for steady-state linear time-invariant behavior.
- Oversampling noise benefits retain the assumptions about noise correlation and measurement bandwidth.
- Differential drive and ADC input-isolation explanations retain the relevant balance and feedback qualifications.
