MOSFETs

Metal-Oxide-Semiconductor Field-Effect Transistor

Active Component Switching

Regions of Operation

Think of a MOSFET like a water valve controlled by pressure.

  • Cutoff: The valve is shut tight. No current flows.
  • Ohmic: The valve is partially open. Acts like a resistor (RDS(on)).
  • Saturation: The valve is wide open. Current is constant regardless of drain voltage.

Behavior defined by VGS and VDS.

1. Cutoff Region
VGS < Vth ➜ ID = 0

2. Ohmic (Linear) Region
VGS > Vth AND VDS < (VGS - Vth)

3. Saturation (Active) Region
VGS > Vth AND VDS ≥ (VGS - Vth)
